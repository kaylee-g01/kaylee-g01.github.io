Meme Mashup

This meme was inspired by the "We are the brothers cat" memes. I wanted to put my own spin on it: Photoshop hotkeys, since I use Photoshop occasionally and sometimes forget the shortcuts for certain things. I think creating the Save As brothers will help me remember to save, so I don't keep losing projects because I don't save my progress. To start, I searched for cat reaction memes, chose three cats, and edited the background from each one. Next, I slapped them all together, adjusting the lighting and contrast on some so they blended well. Then I added text bubbles and filled in their names. Last, I pulled the bottom text from a free Google text generator and added a random "technology" background and the Photoshop logo. Overall, I'm pretty happy with the result.
